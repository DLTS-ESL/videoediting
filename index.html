What this tool can and cannot do
Browsers block most websites from letting other pages read their video sources (CORS) and protect many videos with DRM. This tool cannot break DRM, decrypt protected streams, or freely extract videos from Netflix, YouTube, Disney+, etc. It works best for direct public video file links or for recording what is visibly playing on your screen.

    Realistic Limitations

    • DRM-protected content (Netflix, Disney+, many streaming platforms, some YouTube) cannot be downloaded or captured in usable form by any ordinary website. This is intentional browser and platform security.
    • Cross-origin videos are blocked by CORS. A website cannot freely read video sources belonging to another domain.
    • Blob / MediaSource streams (common on modern players) do not expose a simple downloadable file URL.
    • Screen recording captures pixels only — quality depends on resolution, frame rate, and whether the tab is visible. It is not a perfect source download.
    • Respect copyright and the terms of service of the sites you visit. This tool is intended for videos you have the right to save (your own content, public domain, Creative Commons, etc.).
